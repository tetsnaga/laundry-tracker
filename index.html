Coverage, status interpretation & limits

Machine signal preserves the raw state: 7 = available, 4/1 = running, 5 = finished. Other codes stay unknown. WASH display mirrors the website, which can label an expired countdown or an hour-old report “Available.” Neither can confirm that clothes were removed.

Charts use actual request timestamps, not scheduled times. Each observation represents at most five minutes; missing time is never counted as available. Availability statistics require the full selected machine group to be present and interpretable.

Heatmap cells show the observed percentage of time with at least one available machine, or the average number running. A hatched cell is preliminary: fewer than three dates or 90 observed minutes. Quiet-hour comparisons require at least three eligible hourly slots. Daily outliers require at least four comparable days of the same weekday and 70% coverage between 06:00 and 24:00.

Times are shown in Los Angeles time. Repeated daylight-saving hours overlap on timelines; statistical coverage counts both occurrences. Raw source timestamps are preserved in the CSVs.
